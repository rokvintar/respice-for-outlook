Respice for Outlook

Warns you at send time. Every rule checks To, Cc and Bcc. Settings are stored in your mailbox only.

Rules

Outlook does not tell add-ins Reply from Reply all, so this fires on any reply (detected by Outlook, by the original-message link, or by an RE:/AW:/Odg: subject) with more than one recipient.

Test this message

Runs the saved rules against the message you are composing. Nothing is sent.
